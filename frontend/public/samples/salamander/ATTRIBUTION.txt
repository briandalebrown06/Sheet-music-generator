This application selects one recorded sample layer, pitches neighboring notes
by playback rate, applies velocity-controlled gain and note-off envelopes,
and uses the saved note schedule. It does not redistribute the full multi-layer
SFZ instrument or claim that a sampled review is a human piano performance.
No endorsement by Alexander Holm or Tone.js is implied.

Reproduce/verify downloads with scripts/download_piano_samples.py and the
per-file SHA-256 hashes in scripts/piano_samples.manifest.json.
